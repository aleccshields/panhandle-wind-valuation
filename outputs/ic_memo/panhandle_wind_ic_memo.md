## Executive Summary

Panhandle Wind presents a **HOLD / SELECTIVE BUYER** opportunity at current implied valuations.

The asset carries a strong near-term revenue anchor: 77% of output is hedged at $23.50/MWh through approximately 2027 via a Citibank affiliate structure disclosed in the Pattern Energy Group 10-K. This contracted tranche provides material downside protection during the holding period while the operator manages the post-hedge merchant transition.

At base case assumptions, the asset trades at fair value -- unlevered IRR equals WACC (8.85%), implying zero excess return at the modeled entry price. Levered returns are compelling (26.9% equity IRR on a $13m equity check against $110m of project debt), but this reflects the high leverage ratio rather than intrinsic value creation. The primary risk is capture rate compression: accelerating Texas solar buildout is structurally widening the LZ_NORTH discount to HB_NORTH, and the bear case (-500 bps capture, P90 generation) produces a 0.40x DSCR that cannot service the debt.

**A buyer acquiring at a 20-25% discount to base NPV, with debt sized conservatively on P90 cash flows, would find a more defensible entry structure.**

## Asset Overview

Panhandle Wind occupies a high-quality position in the West Texas Competitive Renewable Energy Zone (CREZ), a region characterized by some of the highest sustained wind speeds in North America. The CREZ transmission build-out (completed 2013) resolved prior bottleneck constraints and enabled reliable delivery to ERCOT load centers.

The asset consists of two separately registered EIA plants (IDs 58242 and 58720), operated under Pattern Energy Group. The aggregate 399.7 MW nameplate is consistent with industry disclosures (218 MW Phase 1, 181.7 MW Phase 2).

## Market Context: ERCOT Capture Rates

The LZ_NORTH / HB_NORTH price ratio (capture rate) is the dominant merchant revenue driver and the most important assumption in the model. Wind generators in West Texas realize a discount to hub prices because:

1. **Wind output correlation with low prices:** High-wind periods coincide with periods of market-wide oversupply, depressing settlement prices.
2. **Solar cannibalisation of midday hours:** Texas solar capacity has grown from ~5 GW (2020) to ~25+ GW (2024), compressing afternoon prices when wind also generates.
3. **Transmission constraints:** LZ_NORTH congestion occasionally widens the basis to HB_NORTH.

**Scenario assumptions:**
- **Base (0.80):** Midpoint of estimated 2022-2024 LZ_NORTH/HB_NORTH ratio
- **Bull (0.83):** Assumes battery storage and demand growth absorb wind-hour oversupply
- **Bear (0.75):** Assumes continued solar penetration further compresses midday prices

The 500 bps bear haircut (-6.25% relative to base) is the single largest value driver in the downside scenario, responsible for approximately 60% of the NPV difference between bear and base.

### Revenue Architecture

**Phase 1 (2025-2027): Partially hedged**

The Citibank affiliate hedge (PEGI 10-K) covers 77% of output at $23.50/MWh. Remaining 23% is merchant.

- Base year revenue: ~$35.5m/yr
- Revenue is relatively stable during this period due to the contracted floor

**Phase 2 (2028-2039): Full merchant**

Post-expiry, all revenue is merchant (hub price x capture rate). Hub price escalates at 2%/yr from a 2024 base of $33/MWh.

- Revenue step-up at expiry: The $23.50/MWh hedge price is *below* the assumed $33+/MWh merchant price, so expiry creates a **revenue increase**, not a cliff -- a favorable structural dynamic for equity holders.
- Average merchant revenue: ~$41-49m/yr (growing through 2039)

### Debt Sizing

Project debt is sized using CFADS sculpting, the standard project finance technique for wind assets:

- **Principal:** $110m
- **Tenor:** 15 years (2025-2039, covering full remaining life)
- **Rate:** 6.03% (Moody's Baa proxy)
- **DSCR floor:** 1.35x (industry standard for investment-grade ERCOT wind)
- **Sizing basis:** P50 CFADS (base case) -- sculpted principal to maintain flat DSCR
- **Avg annual debt service:** $11.6m
- **Leverage:** Debt / avg CFADS = 7.0x

The sculpted structure maximises debt capacity while holding the covenant flat. With debt sized on P50 and a 1.35x buffer, lenders retain a 26% cushion before DSCR breaches covenant, and a 35% cushion before default trigger.

## Key Risks

### 1. Capture Rate Compression (PRIMARY RISK)

Texas solar additions (~15 GW announced for 2025-2027) will continue depressing midday prices. A sustained 25-bps/year decline in LZ_NORTH capture rate (consistent with 2019-2024 observed trend) would erode base-case merchant revenue by $3-5m/yr in the post-2027 period, moving the asset toward the bear scenario. The bear DSCR of 0.40x is not a covenant breach scenario -- it is an insolvency scenario. Any credit buyer must stress-test capture rate assumptions rigorously.

### 2. Post-Hedge Transition Risk (2028)

The modeled revenue step-up at hedge expiry (from $23.50 to $33+/MWh merchant) is favorable under base assumptions but sensitive to ERCOT hub prices. If ERCOT prices settle at 2023-2024 levels (~$25-28/MWh), the "step-up" inverts to a step-down. An acquirer should build a view on ERCOT forward prices across the 2028-2035 window.

### 3. Sub-P90 Generation (2023-2024)

Two consecutive years at or below the empirical P90 threshold raises the question of whether recent generation reflects temporary weather variability (La Nina / drought years affect Texas wind), turbine aging, or increased wake interference from nearby wind development. A prudent buyer would commission an updated energy assessment from a bankable independent engineer.

### 4. Debt Refinancing Not Required

With a 15-year debt tenor matching remaining asset life, there is no refinancing requirement -- but there is also no residual asset value. The levered IRR assumes equity recovery purely through operating cash flows. Any downside scenario reduces equity distributions without any terminal value backstop.

### 5. Hedge Counterparty Risk

The Citibank affiliate hedge represents a bilateral contract. An early termination (regulatory, counterparty credit event, or force majeure) would immediately expose the full output to merchant pricing. Buyers should obtain and review the hedge agreement terms.

## Investment Recommendation

**PASS at base valuation. SELECTIVE BUY at a 20-25% discount.**

At the modeled entry price ($124m NPV), an all-equity buyer earns exactly their required return and no more. Levered equity returns (26.9%) are attractive in isolation but depend on a capture rate assumption (0.80) that is directionally optimistic given the structural trend.

**Entry discipline matters most here.** At a 20% discount (~$99m):
- The asset is under-leveraged at current debt ($110m > $99m)
- Debt would need to be sized down to ~$88m (P90 CFADS basis at 1.35x)
- Equity check: ~$11m against $88m debt
- Unlevered IRR at $99m entry: ~10.5% (210 bps above WACC)

This structure provides meaningful downside protection: the bear case ($46m NPV) is still painful but equity losses are bounded by a smaller initial check, and the credit structure sized on P90 CFADS would maintain DSCR above covenant even under bear assumptions.

**Recommended holding period:** 2025-2032 (capture the contracted period + early merchant years; exit before terminal degradation of capture rates).
